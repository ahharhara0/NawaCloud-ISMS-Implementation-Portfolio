# Risk Assessment and Treatment Method

## Assessment
Each record identifies an asset, event, threat and enabling condition, likely consequence, existing assumed safeguards, likelihood, impact, inherent score, treatment, owner, due date, related controls, closure test, and residual target.

Likelihood and impact use a 1–5 qualitative scale. Score = likelihood × impact: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Medium, 10–16 High, and 17–25 Critical. Likelihood ranges from rare to expected during the planning cycle; impact considers confidentiality, integrity, availability, obligations, and trust. This simplified training model should be calibrated to real business data and continuity needs.

## Treatment options
- **Mitigate:** reduce likelihood or impact through safeguards and test their operation.
- **Avoid:** stop the activity or feature that creates unacceptable exposure.
- **Share:** allocate some impact contractually or through insurance while retaining oversight.
- **Accept:** document an authorized decision, rationale, validity period, and review date.

## Illustrative risk appetite
Critical risks require immediate escalation and a treatment plan. High risks require documented General Manager approval to accept and a dated action plan. Medium risks are scheduled and reviewed quarterly. Low risks are monitored annually. An actual incident or material change reopens assessment.

## Closure
A policy or configuration alone does not close an action. Closure requires owner verification, dated evidence, a suitable sample, restore test, or exercise, a recorded result and exceptions, residual-risk recalculation, and authorized acceptance.
