# Internal Audit Program (Simulation)

## Purpose and independence
The program evaluates whether the designed ISMS arrangements are suitable and whether controls operate as described. In a real organization, management approves the program and appoints a competent auditor who does not review their own work; a small team may require independent external review.

## Annual cycle
| Quarter | Focus | Example procedures | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Scope, assets, risks, SoA | Trace a risk to asset, treatment, control, and exception approval | Workpaper A-01 |
| Q2 | Identity, workforce, suppliers | Sample simulated access and lifecycle records; review supplier assessment | Workpaper A-02 |
| Q3 | Development, change, logging | Trace change from request through review, testing, and release; inspect test alert | Workpaper A-03 |
| Q4 | Backup, incidents, continuity, improvement | Review simulated restore; trace tabletop incident; follow prior actions | Workpaper A-04 |

Additional audits may follow a major incident, structural change, or contractual request. Sample sizes depend on risk and population; a small sample should not be generalized without justification.

## Findings and evidence
Suggested training categories are improvement opportunity, minor nonconformity, and major nonconformity. Each finding should record criteria, facts, impact, root cause, owner, action, due date, and closure evidence. The evidence folder contains fictional references only. No real operational records, user identities, contact details, credentials, or customer data are included.
